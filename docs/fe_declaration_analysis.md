## 5. What E20 blending does to the declared petrol fleet

Mean declared petrol FE across all 188 petrol models is **16.67 kmpl**. Applying the model's E20 mileage-drop band:

| E20 drop | Effective FE (kmpl) | FE lost |
|---|--:|--:|
| 2% (SIAM low) | 16.33 | −0.33 |
| 4% (central) | 16.0 | −0.67 |
| 6% (ARAI high) | 15.67 | −1.00 |

Because petrol is the single largest slice of both new sales and the on-road parc, this small per-vehicle haircut is what aggregates into the national ethanol-penalty figure in the `FleetSales` sheet.
